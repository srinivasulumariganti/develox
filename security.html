Application security

Responsible disclosure

Security is one of our services, so we take reports about our own site seriously.

How to report

Email contact@develox.in with the subject “Security report”. Include the affected URL, steps to reproduce and the potential impact. Please do not include sensitive personal data. Our machine-readable contact is in security.txt.

What we ask

  • Give us reasonable time to investigate and fix before you disclose publicly.
  • Test only against your own accounts and data; avoid service disruption, spam, social engineering and physical attacks.
  • Stop and tell us immediately if you encounter personal data.

What to expect

We will acknowledge good-faith reports, keep you informed, and credit you if you wish once the issue is fixed. We will not pursue legal action against researchers who follow this policy.

This site is a static website: it has no user accounts or database. The contact form transmits your message over HTTPS to our email provider.
