Doctor's Diet Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Doctor's Diet

Last Updated: October 2026

Developed & Operated by: Develox Innovations IT Solutions LLP (develox.in)

Core Principle: Doctor's Diet respects your privacy and personal health autonomy. We never sell your personal or health data. You retain complete ownership of your dietary profile, and you may permanently delete your data and account at any time directly inside the app.

1. Information We Collect

To provide personalized clinical and lifestyle dietary plans, Doctor's Diet processes:

  • Account Details: Name and email address provided during Google OAuth authentication.
  • Dietary & Health Profile: Age, gender, height, weight, activity levels, clinical conditions (e.g., diabetes, hypertension, PCOS), dietary restrictions, food allergies, and affordable food preferences.
  • Consultation & Progress Logs: Chat messages, meal completion status, hydration intake logs, and food nutrition analysis records.
  • Voice Input (Optional): Audio captured via your microphone when you choose to use the speech-to-text consultation feature.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate tailored nutritional guidance, macro estimations, and daily meal schedules.
  • To sync your active meal progress across your verified web and mobile sessions.
  • To provide optional meal and hydration reminders via your device's native clock/alarm services.

3. Third-Party Services & AI Processing

Doctor's Diet utilizes industry-standard, secure infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Manages secure user login tokens and encrypted cloud storage for your synchronized diet progress.
  • Artificial Intelligence Services: Natural language processing and meal suggestions are powered by advanced AI models. Queries are transmitted over HTTPS to authorized inference endpoints. User prompts are processed to generate nutritional responses and are not used to publicly train models.
  • Speech Recognition & Synthesis: Voice transcription relies on your operating system or browser's native speech engine (such as Google Speech Services or Apple Speech). Audio is not permanently stored by Doctor's Diet.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Local Encryption: On mobile devices, local app databases are protected at rest with AES-256 encryption, with keys anchored in hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Transit Encryption: All client-to-cloud communications strictly enforce modern TLS/HTTPS encryption. Cleartext network traffic is disabled.
  • Sign-Out Protection: Signing out of Doctor's Diet automatically clears all local health records from the device to protect shared devices.

5. Medical Disclaimer

Doctor's Diet provides informational nutritional assistance and guidance. It is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis, personalized clinical treatment, or prescription advice. Always seek the advice of your physician or qualified clinical dietitian before making significant changes to your diet or medical treatments.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (India), GDPR, and global data privacy standards, you have the right to:

  • Access your stored dietary history and plans within the app.
  • Sign out at any time to clear local cached data.
  • Permanently Delete Everything: Use the "Delete Account & Data" button in the user profile menu to immediately and irreversibly delete your Firestore records, authentication profile, and local device storage.
